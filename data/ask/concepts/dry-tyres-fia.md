# Dry-weather tyre specifications (FIA)

At each Competition the appointed tyre supplier must provide three specifications of dry-weather tyre, one specification of intermediate tyre, and one specification of wet-weather tyre (Art. 30.1a)i). The Sporting Regulations do not assign the marketing names SOFT / MEDIUM / HARD or the Pirelli C1–C6 codes; those labels are supplier/timing usage. ARIS timing ingest (FastF1) only sees SOFT / MEDIUM / HARD, not C1–C6 (docs/how-recommend-works.md).
