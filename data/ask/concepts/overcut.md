# Overcut mechanics

An overcut is the opposite tactic: stay out while a rival boxes, hoping clean air and a lighter fuel load on the current stint offset their fresh-tyre out-lap. ARIS does not have a separate overcut bonus term. Stay-out is always kept on the scored shortlist even when every pit looks better, so the engineer can reject a pit push. Whether an overcut actually pays is a simulate() ranking result, not a named rule.
