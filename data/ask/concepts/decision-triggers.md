# When ARIS speaks

recommend() is not on a timer. The live engine asks it when a trigger fires: lap 1 (confirm the pre-race plan); tyre life crossing 25% / 50% / 75% of race distance; the car ahead inside the undercut window; gap ahead under 1 s (tactical / line); safety car / VSC. Phase G walk-forward scores a subset of those moments — pit / SC / compound inflections in the real race — not every trigger.
