# Safety Car deployment

The clerk of the course may deploy the FIA safety car to neutralise a sprint session or a race when competitors or officials are in immediate physical danger on or near the track but the session does not need to be suspended (Art. 55.3). On deployment the official message is “SAFETY CAR DEPLOYED”, FIA light panels show “SC”, and marshal posts show waved yellow flags and “SC” boards (Art. 55.4). This is a paraphrase of the 2025 Issue 5 text, not a substitute for the regulation.
