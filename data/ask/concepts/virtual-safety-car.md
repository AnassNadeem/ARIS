# Virtual Safety Car (VSC)

The VSC may be initiated by the clerk of the course to neutralise practice, a sprint, or a race, typically when double-waved yellows are needed but a full safety car is not (Art. 56.1). The official message is “VSC DEPLOYED” and light panels show “VSC” (Art. 56.2). During a sprint or race, a car may enter the pits under VSC only to change tyres (Art. 56.4). Cars must stay above the FIA ECU minimum time once in each marshalling sector and at both safety-car lines (Art. 56.5). Overtaking on track is generally forbidden with listed pit-line exceptions (Art. 56.6). Ending: “VSC ENDING”, then panels go green between 10 and 15 seconds later; drivers may race immediately; greens extinguish after 30 seconds (Art. 56.7). VSC laps count as laps (Art. 56.8).
