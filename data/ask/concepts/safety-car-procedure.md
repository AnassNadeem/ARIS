# Safety Car in-race procedure

Cars must reduce speed and form up in line behind the safety car no more than ten car lengths apart, and stay above the FIA ECU minimum time in each marshalling sector until they have crossed the first safety car line for the second time (Art. 55.7). Overtaking on track is generally forbidden until a car passes the Line after the safety car has returned to the pits, with listed exceptions including pit entry/exit relative to the safety-car lines (Art. 55.8). Other than when cars are required to use the pit lane, a car may enter the pits while the safety car is deployed only to change tyres (Art. 55.12). Laps behind the safety car still count as race/sprint laps (Art. 55.16). Restart: “SAFETY CAR IN THIS LAP”, orange lights out, leader may dictate the pace (Art. 55.15).
