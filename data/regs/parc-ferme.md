# Parc fermé

After qualifying, cars are under parc fermé conditions: setup changes are heavily restricted before the race. Tyres used to start the race are nominated under parc fermé / tyre-start rules for that Competition. ARIS does not simulate parc fermé legality; it only scores remaining-race time from the live compound and tyre life on the car.
