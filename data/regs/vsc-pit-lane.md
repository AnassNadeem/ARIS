# Virtual Safety Car (VSC) pit lane rules

The VSC may be initiated to neutralise a session when double-waved yellows are needed but a full safety car is not (Art. 56.1). Official message: “VSC DEPLOYED”. During a sprint or race, a car may enter the pits under VSC only to change tyres (Art. 56.4). Cars must stay above the FIA ECU minimum time in each marshalling sector (Art. 56.5). Overtaking on track is generally forbidden with listed pit-line exceptions (Art. 56.6). Ending: “VSC ENDING”, then green panels. VSC laps count as laps (Art. 56.8).
