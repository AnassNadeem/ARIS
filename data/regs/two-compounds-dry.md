# Two dry-weather tyre specifications (FIA Art. 30.5)

Unless the race is declared wet, each driver must use at least two different specifications of dry-weather tyres during the race. A wet-declared race lifts that two-specification obligation. This is a paraphrase of Article 30.5 of the 2025 FIA Formula 1 Sporting Regulations, not a substitute for the published text. ARIS timing ingest only sees SOFT / MEDIUM / HARD labels, not C1–C6 codes.
