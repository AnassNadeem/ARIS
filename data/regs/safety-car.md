# Safety Car procedure

The clerk of the course may deploy the FIA safety car when competitors or officials are in immediate physical danger but the session need not be suspended (Art. 55.3). Message: “SAFETY CAR DEPLOYED”. Cars form up behind the safety car no more than ten car lengths apart (Art. 55.7). Other than when cars are required to use the pit lane, a car may enter the pits while the safety car is deployed only to change tyres (Art. 55.12). Laps behind the safety car still count (Art. 55.16). Restart: “SAFETY CAR IN THIS LAP”.
