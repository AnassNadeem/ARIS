# Pit lane layout and speed limit

The pit lane is split into a fast lane (closest to the pit wall, no more than 3.5 m wide) and an inner lane (closest to the garages). Work on a car is done in the inner lane except in listed cases (Art. 34.4). A speed limit of 80 km/h applies in the pit lane for the whole Competition unless the Race Director amends it (Art. 34.7). Practice/qualifying speeding is fined; sprint/race speeding can draw Article 54.3 penalties. ARIS does not simulate those fines.
