# Dry-weather tyre allocation

At each Competition the appointed tyre supplier must provide three specifications of dry-weather tyre, one specification of intermediate tyre, and one specification of wet-weather tyre (Art. 30.1a)i). The Sporting Regulations do not assign the marketing names SOFT / MEDIUM / HARD; those labels are supplier/timing usage. ARIS sees SOFT / MEDIUM / HARD from FastF1, not C1–C6.
