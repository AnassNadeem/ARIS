# Red flag / suspended race

When a race or sprint is suspended (red flag), cars proceed to the pit lane. A free tyre change is typically available during a suspension — the strategy resets because remaining race distance and tyre obligations are re-evaluated at the restart. This is a paraphrase of the 2025 sporting regulations on suspended races (Art. 57), not a substitute for the published text. ARIS treats RED_FLAG as a strategy-reset phase in the race-control FSM.
